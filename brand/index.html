Mascot

The eagle from the mark, with a body so it can act. The head is the mark's own geometry; a navy sticker outline keeps the white head visible on white cards, and on dark backgrounds the outline turns pale (--l4m-line; mascot/<pose>-dark.svg). Below 48 px use the logo mark or an icon instead of a pose. In the app: <Mascot pose="looking" size={120} /> from web/src/components/Mascot.tsx. Every pose blinks every few seconds and has its own motion, and all of it stops under prefers-reduced-motion. Rebuild these files with node brand/mascot/render.ts.

The five concepts, scored

Why Eagle Eye won: it was the only concept that answers “eagle eye” literally, and the most legible at 16 px. Binocular Bird was cuter, so the final grafts its charm onto the eagle: a feather tuft, a rounder eye with a lens glint, a blush, a softer brow. A sky-blue disc replaces the navy one that disappeared on dark backgrounds, and the favicon drops the details that blur below 32 px.
